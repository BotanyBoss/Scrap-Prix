# Scrap Prix — Armoured Tub (Chassis 08/10)

**Concept status:** Pending visual exploration, NOT approved.  
**Catalogue ID:** `armoured-tub`  
**Game role:** Weight 14; power 0 / grip 1 / armour 10 / cooling 0; rough-momentum bonus +0.18. Original catalogue description: "Low-sided armour with all the grace of municipal infrastructure."

## Silhouette first

A **low, broad, self-supporting armoured tub shell**, like a scrapyard bunker or truncated steel bath on a vehicle platform. Its defining visual is a massive enclosed perimeter of heavy riveted steel around a deeply recessed single-seat cockpit. This is a CHASSIS, not an equipped vehicle.

- **Wide and squat** in front and rear views, with high, thick body sidewalls relative to the seated driver but a **low overall silhouette**; no tall truck cab.
- Sloped overlapping armour cheeks, blunt short wedge nose, high shoulders, and thick stepped rear bulkhead.
- Deep protected cockpit aperture with heavy rim, metal reinforcing ribs, and harness mounting points.
- A structural tub/monocoque with continuous armour skirts and cutouts for wheel hubs; no open ladder frame, no thin rails or roll hoop dominating the shape.
- Low, broad rear face with a distinctive inset recess for equipment, clear from the slightly elevated rear chase camera.

## Chassis-only visual requirements

Hero 3/4 view + consistent side, front, elevated rear and top views, plus detail of cockpit rim, structural wall layers, wheel mounting bays and the empty modular engine / gadget attachment spaces.

**Do not include:** wheels, tyres, engine, gadgets, mounted lights, permanent exhaust, cargo, roof rack, tow rig, accessories, or fixed weapons. The clean chassis should stand on invisible supports or a neutral plinth if necessary; avoid making it appear assembled.

Clean baseline retains mismatched scavenged plating and a little workshop wear, but most damage is tied to the durability system.

## Critical differentiation
- **Hauler Frame 07:** exposed heavy ladder rails, upright work-truck nose and stepped rear framework. **Armoured Tub is a solid enclosing shell, not that vehicle.**
- **Boilerplate 04:** open cylindrical engine housing and industrial hot rod. **Armoured Tub has no cylinder.**
- **Salvaged GT 01:** rounded sporty coupe. **Armoured Tub should look like municipal engineering, not a car body with extra armour.**
- **Wobble Wagon 05:** lopsided, structurally uneven wagon. **Armoured Tub is low and symmetric.**

## Candidate explorations

1. **Bunker Bath:** scalloped steel sidewalls, square-cut cockpit slot, two massive trapezoid front shoulders and wide rear ramp-like mass.
2. **Road Roller Tub:** cast industrial trough with flared corner guards, riveted overlap panels and a blunt flattened prow.
3. **Scrap Citadel:** angular tapered tub, fortress-like layered skirts and deep equipment recesses, still low rather than tall.
