# Scrap Prix — Needle Frame (Chassis 03/10)

**Decision (9 November 2026): Visual direction accepted, with a reference consistency caveat.**
**Catalogue ID:** `needle-frame`
**Source:** Third-round cleaned-up Needle Frame concept sheet following selection of the long narrow rat-rod concept. The reference image must be archived with the asset library for production handoff.

## Locked identity
- Long, sharply tapered nose and elongated, very narrow central chassis/spine.
- Light, exposed tubular/trussed frame and minimal body panels.
- Open, single-seat cockpit and roll hoop.
- Clearly different from the broader, more homemade Soapbox Frame and the future enclosed aerodynamic Aero Pod.
- Scrapyard construction and light cosmetic wear at 100% durability.
- Engine, wheels, exhaust/gadget parts remain interchangeable, not baked into the chassis master.

## Important modelling caveat
**The images from different views do not agree.** The user accepted the overall design notwithstanding this mismatch. Do not treat each angle as accurate orthographic drawings. Use the large three-quarter hero design as the primary shape reference, then establish the actual geometry and consistent front/side/rear/top projections from ONE 3D master mesh. Do not force incompatible details together simply to mimic every illustration.

## Outstanding work
- Resolve wheelbase/track width, nose and frame proportions, mounts, rear layout and cockpit/roll-hoop geometry in the 3D engineering pass.
- Check compatibility with unusual engines, tyres and gadgets while preserving narrow chassis readability.
- Verify readability and stability from the straight rear race camera.
- Validate clean vs damaged materials and export pristine aligned GLB.
- No gameplay, save, economy, recipe or live game change implied.

**Production status:** Visual direction accepted / engineering & 3D model pending.
