# Scrap Prix — Longbody Frame (Chassis 06/10)

**Status:** 3D silhouette / chassis-only study; **not visually approved**.
**Catalogue ID:** `longbody`.

## Defining requirement: SIX WHEELS

- **Two wheels at the front** (one front axle).
- **Four wheels at the back**, arranged on **two tandem rear axles**.
- This is part of Longbody's permanent chassis geometry, not optional equipment.
- The chassis-only master must show **three empty wheel-arch openings and mounting positions per side**: one front and two rear.
- The normal single `tyres` equipment slot supplies the tyre type to every wheel station; do not introduce extra gameplay slots.
- Preserve visibly distinct tandem rear axles, long structural rails, realistic clearance and space for interchangeable tyre sizes.

## Body identity
- Low, stretched GT / long-wheelbase grand-tourer character, not a wagon/pickup.
- Reinforced, extended side rails and a stable speed-focused silhouette.
- A prolonged coupe cabin with front hood, rear quarters and coherent roofline.
- Engine and gadgets remain interchangeable, no permanently built-in powertrain.
- This is a **3D blockout** only; final art/finish remains to be designed and approved.

## Reference limitation
The initial 6 November–9 November 2026 image-blockout omitted one rear axle; it is **incorrect** as an asset specification. A revised VTK prototype showing two tandem rear axle clearances per side was rendered on 9 November 2026. Its proportions and panel fit remain provisional, and the illustrative render itself should not be treated as approved art.

## Engineering gates
- Validate wheel collision, ride height, three mounting stations per side, and steering/front vs tandem rear configuration.
- Check rear camera visibility and modular equipment compatibility.
- Export chassis clean of demo damage and wheels after visual and dimensional approval.
- Existing gameplay, saves, economy, recipes and production assets remain unchanged.
