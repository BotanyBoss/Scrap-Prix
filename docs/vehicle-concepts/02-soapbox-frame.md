## Approved design

- Keep the **current complete body shape**, **including its relatively substantial bonnet**. It makes the vehicle read as a small home-built car when rotated in the 3D garage and is *not* a reason for another redesign.
- Open, minimally protected cockpit and distinctive **wooden bucket seat**.
- Exposed, simple roll hoop and minimal tubular/rectangular frame; lightweight improvised construction is the core gameplay/readability identity.
- Patchwork timber and metal panels, with clean repair details at **100% durability**.
- Thin/light wheel arches and an understated front bumper/headlamp mounting arrangement, as in the latest refinement.
- Exposed chassis structure and suspension mounting areas for the modular wheel system.
- Keep the rear view recognisable as a lightweight open-cockpit home-built racer.

## Exclusions / optional cosmetics

- Racing numbers, stars, stickers and similar markings are **optional liveries**, not permanent chassis geometry.
- Engines, tyres and gadgets remain interchangeable, and must not be baked into the chassis master.
- Durability damage is applied by materials/overlays, **not** permanently baked into the 100% clean asset.
