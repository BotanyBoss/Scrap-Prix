# Scrap Prix — Hauler Frame (Chassis 07/10)

**Concept status:** Exploration pending. No approved artwork.  
**Catalogue ID:** `hauler`  
**Game role:** Heavy commercial-grade chassis, power +1, armour +8, cooling +1, weight 13; rugged-road momentum bonus. These are design inputs, not requests to alter gameplay.

## Visual identity

A short, broad **four-wheel industrial workhorse**, midway between a stripped work truck and a deliberately improvised rally machine. The chassis should look like it was originally built to haul unreasonable loads and now has to survive races. Strong forward cab, wide structural shoulder rails, thick steel ladder frame, open and modular rear structure.

**Do not confuse with existing chassis:**
- Not Wobble Wagon: straight/stable heavy commercial geometry, not structurally asymmetric.
- Not Longbody: one front axle and **one rear axle**, not six wheels/tandem rears.
- Not Boilerplate: no riveted cylinder surrounding the engine.
- Not Armoured Tub: structural strength from frame rails and gussets, not a fully enclosed bunker.
- Not Soapbox/Needle: no thin lightweight tubular racer silhouette.

## Chassis-first deliverables

Create *concept art*, not a low-quality procedural blockout. Use the cinematic high-detail **Boilerplate concept sheet** as the presentation-quality reference **only**, not as a body-shape reference.

Show: one large three-quarter hero, clean matching side/front/rear views, exposed chassis structure close-ups, and a small clean neutral-clay bare-shell view. To prove the core asset is modular, the **bare chassis** has no wheels, engine, gadgets, cargo, rooftop gear or permanent exhaust. Four mounting positions are sufficient: one wheel at each corner, independently swappable through the tyre slot.

Design features to explore:
- Short, blunt industrial nose with removable face / open engine space.
- A compact, slightly forward-set cab or racing cockpit with a strong angular windscreen frame.
- Massive box-section rails, steel crossmembers, bolted gussets and heavy suspension attachment points.
- Recognisable *rear shape* in the slightly raised rear race camera: two broad uprights or cutaway shoulders framing an open central rear equipment bay.
- Rich patchwork colour and different steels, but mild cosmetic wear on the pristine master.
- High ground clearance without turning into an expedition vehicle.

## Three divergent concept candidates

**A — Yard Tug:** Compact, square cab, short nose, broad open deck rails, rugged utilitarian bulldog silhouette.

**B — Industrial Rally Hauler:** Slightly lower, raked cab, exposed high-tensile side trusses, road-racing proportions with a very heavy frame.

**C — Recovery Rig Skeleton:** Wider rear frame hoop and thick reinforced crossmembers; visibly built for hauling, but without permanent crane, hook, lift mechanism or cargo.

The artwork can differ radically from earlier 2D art; retain the *gameplay identity*, not an exact reproduction.

## Evaluation gate

- Distinctive in rear and elevated rear three-quarter camera, not just from the front.
- Four wheel mounting positions, **not** six.
- Empty engine and gadget bays; interchangeable equipment must remain visually important.
- Neither a complete truck with fixed cargo nor another light buggy.
- Do not approve automatically based on this brief. User selection/refinement required.
